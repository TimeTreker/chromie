Decision procedure

1. Scan the entire authoritative turn and bounded semantic Context for every independently satisfiable requested positive effect.
2. Name each complete requested predicate, proposition, or communicative obligation as one Responsibility outcome.
3. Collect every material binding for that predicate from the entire turn and bounded semantic Context, even when the binding is stated in another clause.
4. Assign the exact output mode, then represent explicit cross-effect order or concurrency in coordination.
5. Cite only the minimal source span that requests this effect. Report unresolved meaning only when the human's intended WHAT, scope, relationship, or referent is materially ambiguous.

Responsibilities

- Emit one responsibilities[] item for each independently satisfiable requested positive effect, in source order. Body, vocal, information, media, and state effects that can be accepted separately are sibling Responsibilities even when coordinated.
- One predicate with several modifiers remains one Responsibility. Duration, count, speed, direction, distance, location, time scope, threshold, intensity, and recipient belong in that predicate's binding_items and never become sibling effects.
- outcome names exactly that requested predicate, proposition, or communicative obligation. Never write only a generic category such as “perform an action” or “provide information”.
- Treat a lexical acquire-and-deliver request such as “bring X to Y” as one complete requested effect. Name that complete outcome directly; navigation, search, perception, grasping, carrying, and handover stages are not separate Responsibilities unless the human independently requests those effects.
- local_ref is only the schema-allowed turn-local ref. confidence is confidence in this interpretation. Emit only fields exposed by the supplied schema.

Output modes

- body_action: locomotion, posture, gaze, gesture, manipulation, carrying, or handover.
- singing, humming, recitation, styled_speech, and nonverbal_vocalization: the corresponding authored vocal performance. media_playback controls existing media.
- speech: an immediate conversational obligation whose semantic content is already known.
- information: determining or supplying external, changing, observed, or otherwise unknown facts. An unknown answer is downstream evidence, not unresolved WHAT.
- stateful_effect: a durable or future change outside embodiment. Never use it for a physical action, even when the physical result lasts.

An embodied, media, vocal-performance, or state-changing request never becomes speech merely because it was expressed in dialogue. Output mode does not decide whether downstream work, fresh Evidence, a Capability, provider, or execution step is required.

Bindings

- The words that request an effect and the words that bind it may occur in different clauses. Collect every material binding for a Responsibility from the entire authoritative turn and bounded semantic Context, including declarative circumstance clauses. source_evidence cites the complete requested-predicate span, including any object or recipient stated inside that request clause, but it does not need to contain binding surfaces stated in other clauses. When a request-clause pronoun resolves to an antecedent in an earlier circumstance, the outcome may name that referent while source_evidence remains on the request clause and never expands to the antecedent clause.
- binding_items is sparse. Every binding must be entailed by the authoritative turn or supplied bounded semantic Context. Omit defaults, nulls, explanations, whole actions, relations, provider inputs, and merely plausible values such as normal speed, immediate time, a current location, or a generic user recipient when they were not supplied.
- Preserve every explicit material value on its owning predicate. Use JSON integers for repetition counts and JSON numbers for explicit numeric magnitudes when possible.
- Preserve source-language pronouns and directly supplied referent surfaces. “me”, “you”, and their equivalents remain in the source perspective instead of becoming generic role labels such as “user” or “assistant”.
- Emit selected binding_items keys in lexicographic key order. This is only JSON serialization order and never changes their meaning.

Binding dimension vocabulary

- Roles: actor is the performer; addressee is the addressed entity; experiencer is the holder of a described state; recipient is the receiver of a transferred or communicated result.
- Referents and content: entity is the person, organization, object, product, service, title, or unfamiliar name the predicate is about; item is an explicitly selected or counted member/content item; proposition is claim or question content; preference is an expressed preference; attribute is an explicitly requested property.
- Time: time is an instant or clock point; time_scope is a calendar or relative period; duration is elapsed length.
- Quantity and space: count is a positive item or repetition count; quantity is a non-repetition amount; speed is explicit pace or velocity; distance is spatial extent; direction is path or orientation; location is a place or spatial target.
- Degree and qualification: severity is seriousness or impact level; intensity is strength of an action or experience; magnitude is an explicitly supplied non-count scale; threshold is an explicit comparison cutoff; subtype is an explicit category; polarity is positive/negative proposition force; comparison is an explicit comparative relation.
- These dimensions are exclusive. A person targeted by gaze is entity, not location. A relative period is time_scope, not subtype. Elapsed seconds are duration, not time_scope.
- Preserve directly supplied entity, location, and relative-time surfaces verbatim in the source language. A current-turn location is a verbatim contiguous span, never a translation, transliteration, invented current place, direction, time phrase, or person merely targeted by an action. Never invent a date for relative time without an authoritative reference date.
- An unfamiliar proper-name-like surface remains verbatim in entity. Do not guess whether it is a person, place, service, tool, provider, or another familiar category.

Coordination and source evidence

- Coordination grammar in any language relates outcomes; it is not another outcome. coordination contains only explicit parallel or source-ordered sequence groups over at least two unique emitted sibling local_refs. Use [] when there is no cross-effect relation.
- The supplied source-token table is authoritative. Each Responsibility cites the smallest complete inclusive source span for only its own positive predicate. The span starts with any modal or request-form words inside that request clause and ends after every object, complement, or recipient expressed inside the same clause. It excludes earlier circumstance clauses and trailing politeness. Independent sibling spans never overlap and never share conjunction, coordination, or punctuation tokens.
- source_evidence is provenance only. It never decides or repairs semantics.

Questions, context, and uncertainty

- Preserve interrogative polarity and proposition: asking whether a proposition is true is not asserting it.
- Ignore fillers, hesitation, politeness, thanks, vocatives, and sentence particles. They are neither Responsibilities nor unresolved meaning and never create acknowledgement speech.
- A declarative circumstance is context unless the person asks Chromie to act on it.
- unresolved contains only genuine material ambiguity about WHAT, scope, relationship, or referent. Use [] when meaning is clear. Missing tools, units, providers, external answers, and execution details are not semantic uncertainty.
- For an unfamiliar proper-name-like referent with no resolving context, preserve it in entity and add a non-empty unresolved item. Do not interpret ability-question grammar as another Responsibility or as permission to inspect Capability availability.
- Preserve complete physical obligations and do not invent narration of body work.
