## Weather information method — Goal Association

Represent a weather Goal as one `kind=information` resource responsibility. Put location, temporal scope, requested weather aspects, comparison, and answer-shape qualifiers exactly once in `resource_responsibility.query_scope`; resource Goals keep top-level bindings empty. For a resolved local day part, use `entity_type=day_part` with one canonical value: `day`, `morning`, `afternoon`, `evening`, or `night`. A relative expression such as “tonight” carries two dimensions, `entity_type=date` with value `today` and `entity_type=day_part` with value `night`; neither dimension replaces the other. Preserve the user's natural wording only in the Goal description. A direct place mention becomes the location binding. Resolve “there,” “that place,” or similar references before this method is applied, using current discourse and scoped referents rather than prior weather evidence.

A correction such as replacing one city with another supersedes the earlier referent for the current Goal; the old result remains historical only. If human-level location or time meaning is ambiguous, preserve the narrowest source-grounded provisional Goal without choosing a value; Fast Planner owns whether and how to clarify. Do not turn provider-recognized spellings or geocoding candidates into semantic bindings.
